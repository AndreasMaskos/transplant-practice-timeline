# Practice changes in US pediatric heart and lung transplantation, 1987-2026

A dated, sourced timeline of changes in allocation policy, donor matching, immunosuppression, rejection management,
mechanical support and registry forms for transplant recipients younger than 18 years in the United States.

Website: https://andreasmaskos.github.io/transplant-practice-timeline/

- `index.html`: the timeline, with heart and lung views that can be combined, filters for verification status and
  importance tier, a search box, and the linked sources of every entry.
- `pediatric-heart.html`, `pediatric-lung.html`: the same page opening on one view.
- `methods.html`: how the timeline was compiled and verified, what the status and tier labels mean, and its limits.
- `data.js`: all entries. `app.js`, `style.css`: the page. `plotly.min.js`: the chart library (MIT license, see
  `plotly-LICENSE.txt`).

Status: draft. Every entry carries its verification status. The site holds published literature and public policy
documents only, no patient data.

Corrections are welcome through the issue page. Please name the entry, the corrected date and a source.

Andreas Maskos, Stanford University
